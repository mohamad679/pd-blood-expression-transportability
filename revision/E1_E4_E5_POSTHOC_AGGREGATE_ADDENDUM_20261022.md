# Post hoc aggregate diagnostics after E1/E4/E5 results were seen

Status: investigator-requested, post-primary, post-E1/E4/E5; **not** part of the timestamped Phase-2 amendment or the confirmatory PPMI test. The original amendment and frozen model artifacts are unchanged. This addendum is to be hashed before the diagnostic runs. All outputs remain private pending PPMI/DPC guidance.

1. From the existing E1 aggregate moments for the final pooled GEO M5 fit, calculate the absolute standardized mean difference of its gene-logit component between GEO training controls and PPMI controls using `|mean_GEO−mean_PPMI| / sqrt((SD_GEO²+SD_PPMI²)/2)`. Do not pool different fitted models. Recompute the **signed** final pooled M5 gene-logit component `Gγ` on the locked PPMI population without refitting, and report its diagnosis AUROC. Higher `Gγ` retains the frozen model's PD direction; no sign flipping or threshold optimization. Report a descriptive 95% percentile CI from 5,000 diagnosis-stratified paired donor bootstrap draws.
2. Rescore the frozen pooled GEO fits for E4 and all three E5 variants on the same locked PPMI rows. Verify each M4/M5 AUROC matches its already reported aggregate point value to `1e−12`. Calculate paired `AUROC(M5)−AUROC(M4)` and a descriptive 95% percentile CI from **the same** 5,000 diagnosis-stratified donor bootstrap draws for all four variants. Use NumPy `SeedSequence([20261014, 41, 0])`; identical donor indices are used for both models and across variants. Retain duplicates as donor groups, never split copies across classes. Report all four variants regardless of sign.

The bootstrap intervals are conditional on the fixed GEO fits and the observed PPMI cohort; they omit GEO training variability. They are descriptive, do not establish equivalence or biological absence, and cannot replace the locked primary estimate. The E4 target-feature choice remains transductive. The results and exact execution date will be disclosed as post hoc. No individual PPMI values, identifiers, predictions, or bootstrap indices are printed or placed in the repository; the aggregate JSON is written only under `~/PPMI_private`.
