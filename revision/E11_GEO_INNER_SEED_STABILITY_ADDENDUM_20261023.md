# E11: post hoc GEO-only inner-seed stability audit

Status: post-primary, post-E5, and post-E6 exploratory methods addendum. This analysis was requested after the E5 variant results and the discrepancy with the earlier panel-exclusion sensitivity were seen. It is **not** a preregistration, cannot change the locked PPMI analysis, and cannot select a preferred model for the manuscript. The sealed Phase-2 amendment remains unchanged.

## Question and fixed design

Quantify how much inner-fold randomness affects selected regularization and cross-cohort GEO discrimination, and compare three tuning conditions under exactly the same folds. The two directions are GSE57475 (PROBE) to GSE99039 (GENEPARK) and the reverse. For each source cohort, use the unchanged donor records and held-out target cohort. Generate 20 independent five-fold, donor-stratified inner partitions, indexed 0–19. Partition `i` is generated once by the frozen `geo.donor_folds` implementation using scope `E11/{source}/seed_{i}/inner`; its scope-derived seed is shared by all three conditions for that source and index. Do not change the outer held-out cohort.

The three conditions are: (1) AUROC tuning with the full eligible gene universe, (2) weighted-deviance tuning with the full eligible gene universe, and (3) AUROC tuning after removing every marker-panel gene from the eligible universe. Use the unchanged E5 `fit_pair` pipeline: M4 baseline first, M5 candidate including the no-gene option, the same per-training-partition label-free MAD selection of at most 1,000 genes, the same initial lambda grid `(0.0001, 0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1)`, rho grid `(0, 0.5, 1)`, boundary expansion limit of two rounds, and unchanged tie-breaking. Refit M4 and selected M5 on the entire source training cohort. Score the unchanged target cohort only once per fitted pair. No PPMI matrix, outcome, prediction, model, or score may be opened or produced by this audit.

## Endpoints and interpretation fixed before execution

The primary descriptive endpoints, separately by direction and condition, are: the 20 selected lambda values (with no-gene treated as a separate category), corresponding rho values, proportion selecting no-gene, and distribution of held-out `Delta AUROC = AUROC(M5) - AUROC(M4)` (median, full range, and interquartile range). Report every seed, including failures with error type, and the successful denominator. Also report the within-seed paired differences in Delta AUROC between conditions; because folds are identical within each seed, these comparisons remove split assignment as a source of *within-seed* differences. They remain descriptive and do not isolate a biological mechanism.

For this audit only, call selection of no-gene **stable** for a condition and direction if at least 16 of 20 attempted seeds select it and no more than two attempts fail. Call lambda selection **stable** if one exact category (including no-gene) occurs in at least 16 of 20 attempts and no more than two attempts fail. Otherwise describe it as seed-sensitive. A stable positive increment requires at least 16 of 20 attempted seeds to have Delta AUROC >= +0.05 **and** median Delta AUROC >= +0.05 in each transfer direction, with no more than two failures per direction. These cutoffs are descriptive conventions, not hypothesis tests or confidence intervals. Report the complete Delta ranges even when a label applies. If more than two attempts fail in any condition/direction, mark that cell not evaluable rather than discarding failures or replacing seeds. No condition will replace the locked model or the primary PPMI result regardless of this audit's outcome.

The result is an audit of this linear, rank-based pipeline under these two GEO transfer settings. It must be labeled post hoc in the manuscript. The exact execution date, input and code hashes, and any deviation from this addendum will be recorded. Only GEO-derived aggregate output may be written under `results/`; individual-level GEO predictions need not be saved.
