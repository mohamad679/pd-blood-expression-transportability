## 2. Incomplete work and reasons

The substantive missing sources are the full 438-record GENEPARK metadata (P1-07), original PPMI counts for the zero-fraction distribution (P1-12), complete PPMI clinical/treatment and genetic-subcohort fields (P1-08), and group-specific pre-QC denominators (P1-09). The current PPMI metadata does not justify assigning every PD participant to a sporadic de novo or mutation-carrier subgroup. These remain unavailable rather than imputed.

The number trace cannot yet certify the manuscript. The 65 unmatched tokens in the private full census occur on manuscript lines 13, 35, 41, 51, 55, 57, 65, 69, 71, 75, 85, 87, 89, 91, 115, 132, 134, 150, 181, 183 and 185; line counts are visible by filtering `match=N` in the private file. They include protocol/software constants, timing and outcome text that the current numerical source index cannot resolve. Sixteen GEO-side unmatched Results/Table tokens are in the public trace. Matching candidate values also require field-by-field source validation. The author chose to defer the full check to Phase 4 rather than extend Phase 1.

## 6. Unexpected findings

The chip proxy's out-of-fold mean AUROC is below chance, so recorded chip does not behave like the previously observed GENEPARK batch proxy under this frozen-fold calculation. The corrected batch×fold analysis has only nine eligible cells of 45, materially limiting precision. One marker gene is assigned to two panels, fully explaining the 172-versus-173 count without altering the locked model. The private candidate metadata and separate collection table permit a precise 30/63 explanation for the 93 missing collection months.
