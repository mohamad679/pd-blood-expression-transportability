## Rationale and scope

The locked external test estimates the incremental discrimination of a GEO-trained gene block over a demographic and cell-marker baseline across microarray and RNA-sequencing platforms. The following *post-lock, exploratory* analyses distinguish candidate explanations for transfer behavior, assess workflow uncertainty, and describe panel behavior. They cannot alter the primary endpoint, locked models, frozen input matrix, population, or the interpretation of the original confirmatory test. Every specified analysis will be reported regardless of sign. No new variant will be selected by PPMI performance. E3 entails new PPMI model training and remains contingent on the approved application scope; if the approval cannot be established, E3 will be recorded as not run rather than silently replaced.

The code environment is Python 3.11.15 using the existing `requirements_glum_v3.lock.txt` (including glum 3.4.1). Source definitions, ranking, class weighting, block penalty, convergence checks, candidate grid, boundary expansion, and model tie rules follow the locked v3.1 GEO pipeline unless explicitly changed below. The master seed is **20261014**. Distinct streams are derived with NumPy `SeedSequence([20261014, analysis_number, repetition])`; no seed is chosen after inspecting outputs. Donor is the resampling and split unit. Draft E1–E10 software baseline commit: `9d70630845bcd6de08dfcdd59b8d5c9006256c13`. This is a private draft code reference, not a registered amendment DOI.

The execution order after approval is E1, E2, E7, E10, E9 if its eligibility condition is met, E3 if its access scope is confirmed, then the GEO phases of E4 and E5. E4/E5 GEO outputs and their code hashes are frozen before either variant scores PPMI. E6 and E8 follow. E1/E2/E7/E9/E10 require only frozen models or measurements and never refit. All outputs remain private until DPC review. Each analysis emits an aggregate JSON report; code, input, and model provenance is kept in private logs and hashes where applicable. Only the minimal private row-level intermediate needed to reproduce an aggregate is retained; row-level files never enter Git or the public amendment.

## E1 — frozen M5 logit decomposition (I-01)

For each scored sample, use the exact frozen M5 preprocessing, coefficient order, and intercept to decompose the linear predictor as `η_i = α + B_iβ + G_iγ`, where `B` includes age, sex, interactions, and six marker scores, while `G` is the selected standardized gene block. The decomposition must reconstruct the locked logit to numerical tolerance `1e-8`; otherwise fail. No refitting or recalibration is allowed. For each GEO outer/transfer fit, summarize its training controls and held-out GEO controls separately; apply **that same fit** to PPMI controls for its cross-platform comparison. Report fold/direction results separately, never pool component values from distinct fits as if they came from one model. The primary final GEO fit additionally summarizes GEO training controls, PPMI controls and PPMI PD. For each group and component report `n`, mean and sample SD. For each scope compare its held-out GEO controls with PPMI controls scored by the *same* scope model using `|μ_GEO − μ_PPMI| / sqrt((s_GEO²+s_PPMI²)/2)`; zero pooled SD is undefined. Compare held-out versus train gene-component SD within that scope. The prespecified reading is: `|SMD| ≥ 0.5` indicates a material difference between populations under the same fitted representation; it cannot isolate platform from case mix. Held-out/train SD ratio `≥ 2` suggests overfitting or scale instability. Both may occur. These are diagnostics, not causal attribution.

## E2 — label-free rank shift (I-01)

Using the identical frozen common gene universe and within-sample rank convention, compute each gene's mean rank over all available samples separately in each GEO source and in PPMI, without diagnosis labels. Report the distribution (median, IQR, 5th/95th percentiles, absolute median and maximum) of `mean_rank_PPMI − mean_rank_GEO-source` for nonzero-coefficient M5 genes, including the gene count. Separately report Spearman correlation of the two vectors of per-gene mean ranks over the entire common universe. The GEO-pooled comparison is supplementary and weights source samples equally by sample count. Fixed interpretation: larger absolute rank displacement and lower whole-universe Spearman indicate representation shift; neither alone proves which platform or biology caused it. No threshold is used to choose genes except in E4.

## E3 — within-PPMI nested CV (I-01; access-scope gate)

If approved scope explicitly covers training on PPMI, fit M4 and M5 from scratch on the locked PPMI population using 20 independently seeded repetitions of stratified, donor-grouped five-fold outer CV. Each outer training partition has stratified, donor-grouped five-fold inner CV. The same single-source training-only MAD filter (up to 1,000 eligible genes), source/class weights, block penalty, lambda/rho grid, boundary expansion, KKT checks, and `1e-6` tie tolerance of v3.1 apply. All scaling, marker reduction, gene selection and hyperparameter selection are recomputed inside each training partition. No participant's donor can appear in both train and validation within a fold. M4 has no penalty or tuning. Each repetition produces the arithmetic mean of its five *paired* fold AUROC differences `AUROC(M5)−AUROC(M4)`; the primary E3 summary is the mean of those 20 repetition means, with their SD, minimum, maximum, and full 20-value distribution. Do not pool scores across outer-fold models for the main summary. Store diagnostics on selected lambda boundaries. The interpretation bands, applied to the 20-repetition mean, are: `Δ ≥ +0.05`, within-RNA-seq signal under this pipeline but not transportable in the locked test; `−0.02 ≤ Δ ≤ +0.02`, no meaningful incremental signal detected even without platform transfer; `+0.02 < Δ < +0.05`, inconclusive small positive increment; `Δ < −0.02`, adverse within-platform increment. Exact endpoints belong to the adjacent inclusive band. These descriptive bands are not hypothesis tests or a new confirmatory endpoint.

## E4 — transductive common-rank universe (I-01)

Before any diagnosis-based refit, compute each gene's absolute difference between the mean within-sample rank in GEO pooled samples and the mean in *all* PPMI samples, both without labels. Sort ascending by this difference with gene ID as the deterministic tie-break; retain `ceil(0.50 × N)` genes from the frozen common universe as eligible for the M5 MAD filter. Existing marker panels and M4 remain intact. Rerun the complete v3.1 M4/M5 fitting and tuning pipeline for both GEO transfer directions. Save both GEO transfers and the exact selected universe, code hash, and model artifacts before scoring PPMI. Score the same frozen PPMI population, without PPMI labels influencing selection or fitting. Report M4, M5 and paired ΔAUROC for both GEO directions and PPMI, including any failure. This analysis is **transductive, non-prospective and exploratory** because unlabeled PPMI features determine the universe. A positive result is hypothesis-generating only; negative and positive outcomes are reported equally.

## E5 — variant V1: weighted deviance and panel-gene exclusion (I-13, I-14)

Replace the inner-tuning objective for gene models with weighted validation log-loss (deviance), using the same training-derived class/source weighting rule, and minimize it; for M5 remove every gene in the union of the six frozen marker panels from the eligible gene candidates *before* training-only MAD ranking. All other preprocessing, grid, KKT checks, boundary expansion, and tie rules remain as in v3.1; a tie within `1e-6` favors baseline, then larger lambda, then larger rho. The M4 baseline and its panel scores are not removed. Execute and save both GEO transfer directions and the frozen variant specification before PPMI scoring, without changing code or choices between stages. Report AUROC, AP, log-loss, and M5−M4 on all three tests. This two-factor variant cannot isolate which of its two changes caused an effect. Every outcome is exploratory and hypothesis-generating only.

## E6 — source/class-stratified workflow bootstrap (I-03)

Draw 200 donor-level bootstrap samples within each GEO source×diagnosis stratum from the locked 381-record GEO development population, preserving the stratum sizes. For each replicate rerun the full training-only preprocessing, inner tuning, boundary expansion, M4/M5 final fits, and frozen PPMI scoring. The same fixed PPMI evaluation population and labels are used for every replicate; it is not resampled in E6. Save the paired `ΔAUROC = AUROC(M5)−AUROC(M4)` per replicate and use the 2.5th/97.5th percentiles as a workflow-uncertainty interval conditional on the available GEO and PPMI cohorts. If the **first 20 attempted replicates, including failed attempts,** consume more than six elapsed hours, cap the run at **100 attempted** replicates; otherwise cap it at **200 attempted** replicates. Runtime excludes waiting for human intervention and is recorded. Failed fits are retained in an error ledger; no replacement replicate is silently substituted. If fewer than 90% of the specified attempts complete, report the interval as not evaluable rather than choosing a different count. E6 is exploratory and differs from the locked conditional bootstrap CI.

## E7 — descriptive metrics and calibration of locked predictions (I-05, I-06, I-22)

Use only stored locked PPMI predictions; do not refit any discrimination model. Reconstruct the primary full-population 5,000 stratified bootstrap draws with its exact seed and sampling algorithm, and verify that regenerated M5−M4 AUROC quantiles reproduce the locked report before using them for E7. The original executor saved quantiles rather than row-index arrays, so this deterministic regeneration is explicitly a parity check, not a claim that indices were saved. Null references are AUROC `0.5`, average precision equal to the observed PD prevalence `p`, Brier score `p(1−p)`, and log-loss `−p log p−(1−p)log(1−p)`. For each M1–M5 compute AP, Brier, and log-loss with 95% percentile bootstrap CIs; for each metric also compute paired M5−M4 differences using identical full-population resamples. Compute paired `AUROC(M4)−AUROC(M1)` in the full population using those draws. For the prespecified matched population, reconstruct its **separate** 5,000 pair-resampling draws from the deterministic matching rule and seed; each draw samples matched pairs with replacement and preserves both classes. Report both 95% percentile CIs. For M4 and M5, calibration intercept and slope are fitted by unpenalized logistic regression of outcome on the fixed score logit for reporting only, with percentile CIs from the corresponding full-population draws; failures/separation are counted and, if more than 10% fail, the corresponding CI is not evaluable. A LOESS calibration curve for M4 and M5 uses fixed span `0.5` and the same full-population bootstrap resamples for a pointwise 95% band over a fixed grid of predicted probabilities from 0.05 to 0.95 in steps of 0.05; sparse grid points with fewer than 20 effective observations are not plotted. The null references and all intervals are descriptive; no second confirmatory threshold is introduced.

## E8 — M4 leave-one-panel-out ablation (I-10)

For each of the six frozen marker panels, omit exactly that panel score from M4 and refit the unpenalized baseline, with training-only standardization and collinearity reduction as in v3.1. Fit on GEO pooled data and in each GEO transfer source; score the two GEO transfer destinations with their corresponding source fits. **Only the pooled GEO ablation fits score PPMI.** Save all GEO fits, transfer results and their hashes before PPMI scoring. Report full M4 and each ablation's AUROC, paired change from full M4, and source-specific standardized coefficients for retained baseline features. There is no penalty, tuning, redefinition of panel genes, or selection of a favored ablation from PPMI results. Interpret a lower AUROC after omission as descriptive evidence that the panel contributed to that specific fitted baseline, not as a causal cell-type effect. Report every panel and direction.

## E9 — sporadic de novo subgroup (I-02)

Run only if the P1-08 source-data audit establishes a nonempty genetic-PD subset with a reproducible genetic-status field and a defined sporadic de novo subset. Otherwise record `not evaluable: genetic status unavailable or no genetic-PD inclusion established`. Rescore *locked* M4 and M5 predictions within the qualifying subgroup, without any refit or recalibration, and report `n` by class, AUROC for each model, and paired M5−M4 with a stratified donor bootstrap 95% percentile CI (5,000 draws, seed stream E9). If either class has fewer than 10 donors, the subgroup AUROC/CI is not evaluable. This is descriptive; no favorable subgroup is chosen after viewing scores.

## E10 — CBC concordance across all same-month PPMI participants (I-10)

Include every PD and control participant in the frozen candidate population with a CBC observation in the same *calendar month* as the blood sample, using the documented same-month, any-visit linkage and its deterministic duplicate rule. Do not restrict to controls or exclude participants based on CBC values; nonnumeric, conflicting, or mixed-unit measurements are recorded as not evaluable. For each of the six frozen panel scores, report the preassigned corresponding CBC comparison and Spearman rho, `n`, and a descriptive 95% donor bootstrap percentile CI (5,000 draws). The preassigned comparisons are neutrophil panel versus neutrophil count and percentage, monocyte panel versus monocyte count and percentage, platelet panel versus platelet count, T- and B-cell panels versus lymphocyte percentage, and erythroid panel versus red-cell count and hemoglobin. Lymphocyte percentage is not a T- or B-specific assay, and red-cell count/hemoglobin do not directly validate reticulocytes. If a named CBC field is absent, report that comparison as not available. No multiple-testing claim is made. Report weak or discordant correlations as fully as concordant ones.

## Deliberately excluded variants (I-15, I-13, I-14)

1. An elastic-net model over all 16,364 common genes is excluded: this adds a third degree of freedom on the only external test and increases overfitting risk with 381 development records. This restriction belongs in manuscript limitations.
2. A supervised gene filter within each training partition is excluded for the same reason; it is a future research question, not a post-lock branch.
3. M2 training on all 438 GENEPARK records is excluded from the model comparison because the additional records have missing age/sex, known batch–label confounding, and do not match the M1–M5 analysis population. The prior P1-07 comparison remains descriptive only.

## Interpretation safeguards and provenance

The original PPMI primary estimate and its frozen-model, conditional interval are unchanged. E1–E10 are post-lock diagnostics, not independent replication. E3 can explain within-platform performance only if approved and run; E4 uses target features and therefore cannot estimate prospective transfer. E5 combines two planned changes. E6 broadens uncertainty to refitting but remains conditional on observed cohorts. E7–E10 are descriptive. Results and failures will be reported in execution order without selective omission. Code, input hashes, seed streams, dependency lock, model hashes, and artifact manifests will be stored privately. Participant-level PPMI rows, identifiers, predictions, and fitted PPMI weights will not enter the amendment or public repositories. This local draft and its code must be checked against the approved application and DPC instructions before registration or real-data execution.
