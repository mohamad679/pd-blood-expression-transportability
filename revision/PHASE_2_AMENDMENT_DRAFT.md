## Rationale and scope

The locked external test estimates the incremental discrimination of a GEO-trained gene block over a demographic and cell-marker baseline across microarray and RNA-sequencing platforms. The following *post-lock, exploratory* analyses distinguish candidate explanations for transfer behavior, assess workflow uncertainty, and describe panel behavior. They cannot alter the primary endpoint, locked models, frozen input matrix, population, or the interpretation of the original confirmatory test. Every specified analysis will be reported regardless of sign. The number of PPMI-scored variants will be stated in the manuscript, and all variants will be tabulated. No new variant will be selected by PPMI performance. E3 entails new PPMI model training and remains contingent on the approved application scope; if the approval cannot be established, E3 will be recorded as not run rather than silently replaced.

Prior to this amendment, post hoc GEO-only diagnostics had already been performed, including a PROBE chip-prevalence proxy, within-batch discrimination checks, and extraction of the locked final-model specifications. Those are earlier analyses, not prospective components of E1–E10. The model-specification audit found that **one of 210 nonzero M5 genes** belongs to a frozen marker panel and **25 of 26** persistent lambda-boundary decisions were at the lower edge. Thus the panel-exclusion factor in E5 was specified after seeing evidence that direct panel-gene overlap in the final M5 fit was small; it is not presented as a blind or confirmatory test. The earlier diagnostics and this amendment will be dated and distinguished in the manuscript.

The code environment is Python 3.11.15 using the existing `requirements_glum_v3.lock.txt` (including glum 3.4.1). Source definitions, ranking, class weighting, block penalty, convergence checks, candidate grid, boundary expansion, and model tie rules follow the locked v3.1 GEO pipeline unless explicitly changed below. The master seed is **20261014**. Distinct streams are derived with NumPy `SeedSequence([20261014, analysis_number, repetition])`; no seed is chosen after inspecting outputs. Donor is the resampling and split unit. A citable software archive identifier will be inserted before any public posting; draft code hashes remain in the private audit record.

After the private hash-only timestamp, a full comparison of private artefacts with their frozen reference hashes, and a separate execution authorization record, the analyses not dependent on the outstanding E3/E9 reply proceed in this order: E1, E2, E7, E10; then the GEO phases of E4 and E5; then their PPMI scoring; then E6 and E8. E3 runs only after its access scope is confirmed; E9 runs only after its access and subgroup eligibility conditions are confirmed. ## E1 — frozen M5 logit decomposition (I-01)

For each scored sample, use the exact frozen M5 preprocessing, coefficient order, and intercept to decompose the linear predictor as `η_i = α + B_iβ + G_iγ`, where `B` includes age, sex, interactions, and six marker scores, while `G` is the selected standardized gene block. The decomposition must reconstruct the locked logit to numerical tolerance `1e-8`; otherwise fail. No refitting or recalibration is allowed. For each GEO outer/transfer fit, summarize its training controls and held-out GEO controls separately; apply **that same fit** to PPMI controls for its cross-platform comparison. Report fold/direction results separately, never pool component values from distinct fits as if they came from one model. The primary final GEO fit additionally summarizes GEO training controls, PPMI controls and PPMI PD. For each group and component report `n`, mean and sample SD. For each scope compare its held-out GEO controls with PPMI controls scored by the *same* scope model using `|μ_GEO − μ_PPMI| / sqrt((s_GEO²+s_PPMI²)/2)`; zero pooled SD is undefined. Compare held-out versus train gene-component SD **only for within-cohort outer fits**, not for cross-cohort transfer fits. The prespecified reading is: `|SMD| ≥ 0.5` indicates a material difference between populations under the same fitted representation; it cannot isolate platform from case mix. Within-cohort held-out/train SD ratio `≥ 2` suggests overfitting or scale instability. Both may occur. These are diagnostics, not causal attribution.

## E2 — label-free rank shift (I-01)

Using the identical frozen common gene universe and within-sample rank convention, compute each gene's mean rank over all available samples separately in each GEO source and in PPMI, without diagnosis labels. Report the distribution (median, IQR, 5th/95th percentiles, absolute median and maximum) of `mean_rank_PPMI − mean_rank_GEO-source` for nonzero-coefficient M5 genes, including the gene count. Separately report Spearman correlation of the two vectors of per-gene mean ranks over the entire common universe. The GEO-pooled comparison is supplementary and weights source samples equally by sample count. Fixed interpretation: larger absolute rank displacement and lower whole-universe Spearman indicate representation shift; neither alone proves which platform or biology caused it. No threshold is used to choose genes except in E4.

## E3 — within-PPMI nested CV (I-01; access-scope gate)

If approved scope explicitly covers training on PPMI, fit M4 and M5 from scratch on the locked PPMI population using 20 independently seeded repetitions of stratified, donor-grouped five-fold outer CV. Each outer training partition has stratified, donor-grouped five-fold inner CV. The same single-source training-only MAD filter (up to 1,000 eligible genes), source/class weights, block penalty, lambda/rho grid, boundary expansion, KKT checks, and `1e-6` tie tolerance of v3.1 apply. All scaling, marker reduction, gene selection and hyperparameter selection are recomputed inside each training partition. No participant's donor can appear in both train and validation within a fold. M4 has no penalty or tuning. Each repetition yields the arithmetic mean of its five *paired* fold AUROC differences, `AUROC(M5)−AUROC(M4)`; report all 20 means, their mean, SD and range as **split variability only**, not a sampling CI. Separately, average each donor's out-of-fold predicted probability over the 20 repetitions for each model. Compute the paired AUROC difference from those donor-level mean predictions and its 95% percentile CI from 5,000 diagnosis-stratified paired donor bootstrap draws. This CI is conditional on the fitted CV models and omits their training uncertainty; score averaging and pooling across fold-specific models also require descriptive interpretation. Store diagnostics on selected lambda boundaries.

In the same ordinary outer folds, score a plate-only prevalence rule: each held-out donor receives the PD prevalence of its recorded plate in that fold's training set, falling back to the training-set overall prevalence if the plate is absent. Report fold AUROCs and repetition means. As a plate-control sensitivity, repeat the full 20-repetition nested M4/M5 CV with recorded plate as the nonoverlapping group in **both outer and inner folds**; report the same paired fold summaries and mean-OOF paired donor-bootstrap CI. If a plate-grouped fold lacks both classes or cannot satisfy the prespecified fit rules, mark this sensitivity not evaluable and retain the reason; do not relax grouping after inspecting outcomes. The plate-only rule is also tabulated on those grouped folds, where unseen plates use the global training prevalence.

The interpretation bands apply to the ordinary CV **mean-OOF paired AUROC difference**, with the plate diagnostics shown alongside: `Δ ≥ +0.05` means an observed within-PPMI increment, **not attributable to biology without plate control**; `−0.02 ≤ Δ ≤ +0.02` supports only the conditional exclusion of an increment of `+0.05` or greater **if the bootstrap CI upper limit is below +0.05**; otherwise it is inconclusive; `+0.02 < Δ < +0.05` is inconclusive; `Δ < −0.02` is an adverse point estimate whose CI is reported without claiming a confirmatory harm effect. Exact endpoints belong to the adjacent inclusive band. If the ordinary mean-OOF Δ is ≥ +0.05 but the plate-grouped mean-OOF Δ is < +0.02, report the ordinary increment as plate-associated, without claiming plate is its sole cause. If both are ≥ +0.05, report an increment that survives plate grouping, still without biological attribution. If the ordinary Δ is ≥ +0.05 and the plate-grouped Δ is between +0.02 inclusive and +0.05 exclusive, report attenuation with an inconclusive plate contribution. If the ordinary Δ is < +0.05, report both estimates descriptively without claiming a positive ordinary increment at that threshold. If the plate-grouped sensitivity is not evaluable, make no plate attribution. These comparison rules use point estimates; report both CIs separately and do not treat threshold crossings as confirmatory tests. No band constitutes a new confirmatory hypothesis test.

## E4 — transductive common-rank universe (I-01)

Before any diagnosis-based refit, compute each gene's absolute difference between the mean within-sample rank in GEO pooled samples and the mean in *all* PPMI samples, both without labels. Sort ascending by this difference with gene ID as the deterministic tie-break; retain `ceil(0.50 × N)` genes from the frozen common universe as eligible for the M5 MAD filter. Existing marker panels and M4 remain intact. Rerun the complete v3.1 M4/M5 fitting and tuning pipeline for both GEO transfer directions. After the two transfer fits, train one final pooled GEO fit using the same selected universe; it is the only E4 model that scores PPMI. Save both GEO transfers, the final pooled fit, and the exact selected universe, code hash, and model artifacts before scoring PPMI. Score the same frozen PPMI population, without PPMI labels influencing selection or fitting. The GEO transfer results are also transductive with respect to each GEO target cohort because the GEO-pooled rank calculation used to select the universe includes that target cohort. Report M4, M5 and paired ΔAUROC for both GEO directions and PPMI, including any failure. This analysis is **transductive, non-prospective and exploratory** because unlabeled PPMI features determine the universe. A positive result is hypothesis-generating only; negative and positive outcomes are reported equally.

## E5 — variant V1: weighted deviance and panel-gene exclusion (I-13, I-14)

Run three fixed variants: **deviance-only**, **panel-gene-exclusion-only**, and **combined**. M4 is fitted once per inner fold without tuning; its validation score serves as the no-gene option when choosing among M5 candidates. The deviance factor replaces AUROC with negative training-weighted validation log-loss as the criterion for this baseline-versus-M5 choice under the same class/source weighting rule. It does not tune M4 hyperparameters. The exclusion factor removes every gene in the union of the six frozen marker panels from M5's eligible candidates *before* training-only MAD ranking. The M4 baseline and its panel scores remain intact in all variants. All other preprocessing, grid, KKT checks, boundary expansion, and tie rules remain as in v3.1; a tie within `1e-6` favors baseline, then larger lambda, then larger rho. Execute and save both GEO transfer directions and the pooled GEO fits for **all three variants** before any PPMI scoring, without changing code or choices between stages. Report AUROC, AP, log-loss, and M5−M4 for every variant in both GEO directions and PPMI; score PPMI only with the pooled GEO fits. The single-factor variants inform which change matters, while the combined result cannot uniquely attribute an interaction. Given the prior audit's one-of-210 overlap, a large effect from exclusion alone is not expected in the locked final fit, but every outcome is exploratory and reported.

## E6 — source/class-stratified workflow bootstrap (I-03)

Draw 200 donor-level bootstrap samples within each GEO source×diagnosis stratum from the locked 381-record GEO development population, preserving the stratum sizes. **Bootstrap duplicates of the same original record retain one donor group ID in every inner split**; no copy may enter validation while another enters training. For each replicate rerun the full training-only preprocessing, inner tuning, boundary expansion, M4/M5 final fits, and PPMI scoring. Compute two paired `ΔAUROC = AUROC(M5)−AUROC(M4)` values from each successful refit: first on the fixed PPMI evaluation population (**training-only uncertainty**); second on a fresh diagnosis-stratified donor resample of that population using the same paired M4/M5 predictions (**training plus PPMI-sampling uncertainty**). The resample is independent of the GEO resample and does not affect fitting. Report means and separate 2.5th/97.5th percentile intervals for both distributions. The second interval includes PPMI evaluation-sample variation but remains conditional on the observed GEO and PPMI cohorts. If the **first 20 attempted replicates, including failed attempts,** consume more than six elapsed hours, cap the run at **100 attempted** replicates; otherwise cap it at **200 attempted** replicates. Runtime excludes waiting for human intervention and is recorded. Failed fits are retained in an error ledger; no replacement replicate is silently substituted. If fewer than 90% of the specified attempts complete, report both intervals as not evaluable. E6 is exploratory and differs from the locked conditional bootstrap CI.

## E7 — descriptive metrics and calibration of locked predictions (I-05, I-06, I-22)

Use only stored locked PPMI predictions; do not refit any discrimination model. Reconstruct the primary full-population 5,000 stratified bootstrap draws with its exact seed and sampling algorithm, and verify that regenerated M5−M4 AUROC quantiles reproduce the locked report before using them for E7. The original executor saved quantiles rather than row-index arrays, so this deterministic regeneration is explicitly a parity check, not a claim that indices were saved. Null references are AUROC `0.5`, average precision equal to the observed PD prevalence `p`, Brier score `p(1−p)`, and log-loss `−p log p−(1−p)log(1−p)`. For each M1–M5 compute AP, Brier, and log-loss with 95% percentile bootstrap CIs; for each metric also compute paired M5−M4 differences using identical full-population resamples. Compute paired `AUROC(M4)−AUROC(M1)` in the full population using those draws. For the prespecified matched population, reconstruct its **separate** 5,000 pair-resampling draws from the deterministic matching rule and seed; each draw samples matched pairs with replacement and preserves both classes. Report **both** matched-population contrasts, `AUROC(M5)−AUROC(M4)` and `AUROC(M4)−AUROC(M1)`, with paired 95% percentile CIs. For M4 and M5, calibration intercept and slope are fitted by unpenalized logistic regression of outcome on the fixed score logit for reporting only, with percentile CIs from the corresponding full-population draws; failures/separation are counted and, if more than 10% fail, the corresponding CI is not evaluable. A LOESS calibration curve for M4 and M5 uses fixed span `0.5` and the same full-population bootstrap resamples for a pointwise 95% band over a fixed grid of predicted probabilities from 0.05 to 0.95 in steps of 0.05; sparse grid points with fewer than 20 effective observations are not plotted. The null references and all intervals are descriptive; no second confirmatory threshold is introduced.

## E8 — M4 leave-one-panel-out ablation (I-10)

For each of the six frozen marker panels, omit exactly that panel score from M4 and refit the unpenalized baseline, with training-only standardization and collinearity reduction as in v3.1. Fit on GEO pooled data and in each GEO transfer source; score the two GEO transfer destinations with their corresponding source fits. **Only the pooled GEO ablation fits score PPMI.** Save all GEO fits, transfer results and their hashes before PPMI scoring. Report full M4 and each ablation's AUROC, paired change from full M4, and a 95% percentile CI for that change from 5,000 diagnosis-stratified **paired donor** bootstrap draws of the fixed predictions in each test population. Report source-specific standardized coefficients for retained baseline features. There is no penalty, tuning, redefinition of panel genes, or selection of a favored ablation from PPMI results. Interpret a lower AUROC after omission as descriptive evidence that the panel contributed to that specific fitted baseline, not as a causal cell-type effect. Report every panel and direction.

## E9 — sporadic de novo subgroup (I-02)

Run only if the P1-08 source-data audit establishes a nonempty genetic-PD subset with a reproducible genetic-status field and a defined sporadic de novo subset. Otherwise record `not evaluable: genetic status unavailable or no genetic-PD inclusion established`. Rescore *locked* M4 and M5 predictions within the qualifying subgroup, without any refit or recalibration, and report `n` by class, AUROC for each model, and paired M5−M4 with a stratified donor bootstrap 95% percentile CI (5,000 draws, seed stream E9). If either class has fewer than 10 donors, the subgroup AUROC/CI is not evaluable. This is descriptive; no favorable subgroup is chosen after viewing scores.

## E10 — CBC concordance across all same-month PPMI participants (I-10)

Include every PD and control participant in the frozen candidate population with a CBC observation in the same *calendar month* as the blood sample, using the documented same-month, any-visit linkage and its deterministic duplicate rule. Do not restrict to controls or exclude participants based on CBC values; nonnumeric, conflicting, or mixed-unit measurements are recorded as not evaluable. For each of the six frozen panel scores, report the preassigned corresponding CBC comparison and Spearman rho, `n`, and a descriptive 95% donor bootstrap percentile CI (5,000 draws). The preassigned comparisons are neutrophil panel versus neutrophil count and percentage, monocyte panel versus monocyte count and percentage, platelet panel versus platelet count, T- and B-cell panels versus lymphocyte percentage, and erythroid panel versus red-cell count and hemoglobin. Lymphocyte percentage is not a T- or B-specific assay, and red-cell count/hemoglobin do not directly validate reticulocytes. If a named CBC field is absent, report that comparison as not available. No multiple-testing claim is made. Report weak or discordant correlations as fully as concordant ones.

## Deliberately excluded variants (I-15, I-13, I-14)

1. An elastic-net model over all 16,364 common genes is excluded: this adds a third degree of freedom on the only external test and increases overfitting risk with 381 development records. This restriction belongs in manuscript limitations.
2. A supervised gene filter within each training partition is excluded for the same reason; it is a future research question, not a post-lock branch.
3. M2 training on all 438 GENEPARK records is excluded from the model comparison because the additional records have missing age/sex, known batch–label confounding, and do not match the M1–M5 analysis population. The prior P1-07 comparison remains descriptive only.

## Interpretation safeguards and provenance

The original PPMI primary estimate and its frozen-model, conditional interval are unchanged. E1–E10 are post-lock diagnostics, not independent replication. E3 can describe within-platform discrimination only if approved and run; its ordinary and plate-grouped estimates may differ, and neither by itself establishes biology. E4 uses target features and therefore cannot estimate prospective transfer. E5 reports both single-factor changes and their combination. E6 broadens uncertainty to refitting and PPMI evaluation sampling but remains conditional on the observed cohorts. E7–E10 are descriptive. Results and failures will be reported in execution order without selective omission. Code, input hashes, seed streams, dependency lock, model hashes, and artifact manifests will be stored privately. Participant-level PPMI rows, identifiers, predictions, and fitted PPMI weights will not enter the amendment or public repositories. The private hash-only timestamp does not constitute public registration or DPC approval. Public release awaits written DPC guidance. Before the first real-data execution, the frozen private artefacts must be fully rechecked against their reference hashes and the comparison recorded in the execution authorization record; E3 and E9 retain their separate gates.
