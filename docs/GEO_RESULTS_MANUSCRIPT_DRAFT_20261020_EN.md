# GEO manuscript draft: Results and Discussion

**Status:** GEO development results only, before the second public lock and PPMI scoring. Table numbering is provisional.

## Results

The development cohorts comprised 142 PROBE participants (93 PD, 49 controls) and 239 GENEPARK participants (120 PD, 119 controls). All five registered models were evaluated in five fixed, donor-separated outer folds within each cohort and in both directional cohort-held-out transfers (Table 1). Within-cohort mean AUROC was 0.561 for the cell-marker baseline M4 and 0.591 for gene-augmented M5 in PROBE, and 0.663 and 0.686 in GENEPARK. The paired fold M5−M4 differences averaged +0.030 (sample SD 0.049; range −0.017 to +0.095) and +0.023 (SD 0.074; range −0.056 to +0.125), respectively. In PROBE→GENEPARK transfer, M4 achieved AUROC 0.667 and M5 0.488 (Δ −0.178; descriptive paired-bootstrap 95% interval −0.236 to −0.121); in the reverse transfer they achieved 0.635 and 0.627 (Δ −0.009; interval −0.089 to +0.071). M1, using age and sex alone, achieved transfer AUROC 0.622 and 0.585. PROBE→GENEPARK transfer log loss was 6.414 for M5 versus 0.664 for M4 (Table 1).

The exploratory 200-replicate, one-sided label-permutation analysis in GENEPARK yielded p=0.004975 for the absolute fold-mean AUROC of M4 and M5, and p=0.233831 for their increment. Technical metadata covered all GEO participants: 16 title-derived chip proxies in PROBE and 11 recorded batches in GENEPARK. GENEPARK batch composition differed by diagnosis; one batch contained 31 PD and 2 controls, another 9 PD and 27 controls. A batch-only prevalence predictor evaluated in the same outer folds achieved mean AUROC 0.691, compared with 0.686 for M5. Among the four GENEPARK batches with at least five participants per class, the unweighted mean within-batch AUROC from saved held-out predictions was 0.655 for M4 and 0.661 for M5 (Supplementary Table S2).

In the prespecified GEO sensitivities, M5 mean within-cohort AUROC after X/Y removal was 0.533 in PROBE and 0.692 in GENEPARK; after train-only age/sex residualization, 0.538 and 0.699; and after removing the 172 panel genes from its candidate universe, 0.563 and 0.684 (Supplementary Table S2). Panel-gene exclusion raised PROBE→GENEPARK M5 transfer AUROC from 0.488 to 0.539, while the unchanged M4 remained at 0.667: the transfer deficit was not explained by direct reuse of panel genes. The matched-population sensitivity had one complete transfer evaluation, GENEPARK→PROBE (46 matched test pairs), with M4/M5 AUROC 0.623/0.632. PROBE matched outer folds and one GENEPARK matched outer fold were non-evaluable under the fixed minimum-size rule; the four evaluable GENEPARK folds are reported separately (Supplementary Table S2).

In the exploratory partial-ComBat sensitivity, inductive correction without a disease covariate reduced GENEPARK within-cohort M5 AUROC from 0.686 to 0.607 and M4 from 0.663 to 0.624. The M5−M4 contrast was −0.017 in that arm, +0.026 in the training-only disease-preserving arm, and −0.003 in the transductive no-disease arm. PROBE→GENEPARK transfer M5 AUROC increased to 0.604 after inductive no-disease correction, but remained below the corresponding corrected M4 (0.655). No ComBat transfer arm showed a positive gene-block increment (Supplementary Table S2). Across the completed GEO disease-model and sensitivity decisions, persistent lambda boundaries occurred in 26 of 265 saved M2/M3/M5 tuning decisions (Supplementary Table S1).

The fixed sex-marker positive control transferred with AUROC 1.000 and 0.993 in the two directions; the separate age predictor did not meet its registered transfer-capacity criterion. Neither control used PPMI outcomes (Supplementary Table S3).

## Discussion

The full-population cohort-held-out analyses provided no positive M5−M4 transfer increment, including the original directional transfers and the complete-cohort sensitivity arms. The only small positive contrast was +0.009 in the separately defined matched GENEPARK→PROBE population; its different label-defined target and small test set preclude treating it as a gain in the original transfer population. The original fold-mean differences were also smaller than the *paired* fold-to-fold SDs. Together, these findings do not show a stable transportable increment from the added gene block over M4.

In GENEPARK, recorded batch alone discriminated PD from controls about as well as the gene-augmented model. Yet within-batch discrimination remained above chance in the four evaluable batches, so the model cannot be described simply as a batch classifier. These within-batch estimates combine predictions from five outer-fold models with potentially different score scales and depend on one high-performing batch. The permutation procedure breaks both disease and batch associations and therefore does not identify disease-specific expression signal. Cross-cohort evaluation limits reliance on cohort-specific batch patterns but cannot assign a causal share of signal to batch, cell mixture, or PD biology.

Panel-gene exclusion does not establish independence from cell composition because correlated non-panel genes can substitute. Age/sex residualization may remove genuine disease-correlated variation along with demographic variation. Partial ComBat runs on within-sample ranks rather than normalized intensities and may remove real PD-associated variation when batch and diagnosis are confounded. The only appreciable positive GENEPARK ComBat increment occurred in the disease-preserving arm, matching the risk identified before outcome review that disease-covariate correction can distort group contrasts under batch–diagnosis imbalance; this remains an interpretive caution, not proof that the observed increment is artifactual. M4 also changes under ComBat because its cell-marker scores are rebuilt from corrected expression. The transductive arm uses held-out expression for adjustment and is not prospective external validation.

Several limits remain material: GEO outer test folds are small; the transfer bootstrap intervals condition on fixed fitted models and do not cover training uncertainty; and boundary lambda selections recur after the permitted two grid expansions, especially in PROBE training scopes. For ComBat, the first-run code hash was not preserved across an interrupted start and retry, and a one-scope numerical recheck captured package divide-by-zero convergence warnings although all checked corrected matrices were finite. These ComBat estimates remain exploratory. The reserved PPMI cohort, after population/plate review and a second public lock, is required for the external evaluation.

## Candidate abstract sentences

Across the complete-cohort GEO transfer evaluations, adding the gene block did not improve discrimination over the cell-marker baseline; a separate age/sex-matched transfer showed only a +0.009 descriptive difference.
