# Final GEO-trained auxiliary sex model: pre-fit execution decision

**Decision date:** 2026-10-20. This implements the five-Y-marker control in the v3.1 protocol. It is written before the pooled GEO control fit and before any PPMI expression-derived score. The two directional GEO control evaluations have already been completed and are not repeated or replaced here.

Use only the healthy controls from the two frozen GEO development inputs. Require their SHA-256 values to match `data/manifests/input_audit.json`, and require one row per operational donor. The pooled final model must not use PPMI expression or labels. The five exact markers are read from `protocol/v3_marker_manifest.json`; all five must exist once in the common gene universe. Scale each marker by the mean and population SD of the pooled GEO controls in the relevant training partition, without MAD selection or an added predictor.

For final tuning, form five shuffled donor-level inner folds stratified jointly by source and recorded sex using `geo_pipeline.donor_folds` with the target set to sex and scope `controls/pooled/sex/inner`. Every source×sex stratum must have at least five donors; otherwise stop. Use uniform control weights, glum 3.4.1 ridge-binomial logit, an unpenalized intercept, all five slopes penalized, and the same `geo_pipeline.fit_checked` KKT backend, retry policy, and numerical settings as the directional controls. Start with the registered lambda grid `{0.0001, 0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1}`. Select by mean unweighted validation AUROC; ties within `1e-6` favor larger lambda. If a selected lambda lies at either edge, extend that edge by one decade, reusing the frozen folds and prior candidate results, for no more than two rounds. Record a remaining edge without further search. Prediction warnings must be attributed to fold and candidate.

Refit once on all pooled GEO controls at the selected lambda, with newly calculated pooled control means and SDs. Save the ordered marker IDs, selected gene indices, means, SDs, coefficients, intercept, code/config/input hashes, fold-membership hash, selected lambda, candidate scores, expansions, convergence diagnostics, and warnings. Model arrays, sample identifiers, and per-fit logs remain in ignored `private_data/`; only an aggregate manifest without individual records may be committed. A final synthetic test must verify marker ordering, fold separation, and artifact reload/prediction parity before the real fit.

This fit is an auxiliary transfer gate for later use in PPMI *controls* after the second public lock. It does not assess PD, change any M1–M5 model, replace the directional GEO gate, or authorize a PPMI score. If fitting, hashing, or KKT validation fails, retain the failure and do not substitute another model after seeing PPMI output.
