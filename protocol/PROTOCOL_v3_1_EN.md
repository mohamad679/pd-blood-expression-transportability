## Question, populations and limits of the claim

Primary question: does adding blood genes to a model containing age, sex and six cell-marker scores increase AUROC in independent PPMI when the model is developed exclusively in GEO? This concerns the predictive value of this procedure under cross-platform transfer; it does not determine the causal contributions of disease/shortcuts, complete independence from cell composition, or clinical benefit of the test.

Development: PROBE/GSE57475 contains 93 PD and 49 controls; GENEPARK-TRO/GSE99039 contains 120 IPD and 119 controls with complete age and sex. Total: 381 development records. The 199 excluded target GENEPARK records and the dependence of missingness on batch will be reported. Operationally, each PROBE GSM is assumed to represent a donor; its independent public identifier is incomplete. Complete independence between studies will not be claimed without a crosswalk/provenance support.

Test: PPMI Project133 IR3-B38, baseline visit, Long RNA, valid QC, valid age and sex, and agreement of historical idiopathic PD/healthy-control labels. The actual recovery report of 15 October showed one-to-one linkage of 4,871 count files to release rows and count linkage for **311 PD and 161 controls**. These 472 people are the currently linked population; documented alias/ambiguity resolution may change the final counts. No model or disease/sex/age/CBC score has yet been computed in PPMI. Group metadata have already been viewed for feasibility, and this is disclosed in the preregistration.

## Inputs and primary transfer

The same 16,364 shared protein-coding genes, based on unique Entrez→GENCODE v29 mapping, are used; 15,720 autosomal genes are available for the X/Y-removal sensitivity analysis. In arrays, valid probes for each gene are aggregated by their median within each sample and transformed within sample to `(midrank−0.5)/G`. For RNA-seq, featureCounts are first divided by reference exon-union length, followed by the same ranking. All shared genes and their lengths have been checked in the IR3 archive.

Primary transfer is **inductive**: feature selection, scaling, residualization, tuning and coefficients come exclusively from GEO training data. No PPMI centering or population statistic enters the PD model transformation. Ranking within a sample does not require other samples. Prior processing of the GEO sources remains a separate limitation; this audit does not start from raw CEL/FASTQ.

## Sample weights and tuning criterion

PD prevalence is 93/142 in PROBE and 120/239 in GENEPARK-TRO. For a source-only score, unweighted pooled AUROC is **1951/3408 = 0.5724765**; this is calculated from aggregate counts, not the performance of a real gene model. To avoid rewarding this prevalence difference in training and model selection, each training record receives `w_i = n/(2*K*n[source_i,class_i])`. K is the number of sources in that training partition and n its number of records. Weights sum to n and each source×class cell has equal mass; both classes must be present in every training source. Weights for **M1 through M5, baseline-only, and all inner, outer, single-source, transfer and final fits** are calculated from the corresponding training partition. With K=1, the same rule balances the two classes. No PPMI weight or prevalence is used in training.

The tuning criterion is the mean **unweighted AUROC within each validation source**, followed by an equal mean across folds. For a single source, this is its within-source AUROC. Pooled AUC is not the selection criterion; training weights are not reapplied to external metrics. Loss is divided by the sum of weights for that fit, so multiplying all weights by a constant does not change the lambda scale. Preprocessing still uses unweighted training means and SDs; outcome-aware weighting occurs only in fitting. Seeds and splits are shared across all models.

Kish=`sum(w)^2/sum(w²)` is reported for every fit and the final fit. Based on development counts, pooled Kish is **334.0463 out of 381**; single-source PROBE is **128.3662 out of 142**, and GENEPARK-TRO is **238.9958 out of 239**. This only summarizes weight concentration; it is not the exact information-based sample size for AUROC/regression or a substitute for external n. Equal source mass downweights the larger source and may reduce precision.

## Models and explicit penalties

All disease models use the **binomial/logit** family with the selected backend **glum 3.4.1**. Official documentation confirms per-feature penalty weights: [P1/P2](https://glum.readthedocs.io/en/latest/glm.html#glum.GeneralizedLinearRegressor).

| Model | Unpenalized baseline | Penalized block |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | Age, age², recorded sex, age×sex interaction | None |
| M2 | Intercept only | Up to 1,000 genes |
| M3 | M1 components | Up to 1,000 genes |
| M4 | M1 components + six cell-marker scores | None |
| M5 | Exactly the same M4 components | Up to 1,000 genes |

If B is the baseline block and G the selected genes, the M5 objective is weighted mean logistic negative log-likelihood, divided by the sum of weights, plus `lambda*rho*sum(abs(gamma)) + lambda*(1-rho)*sum(gamma²)/2`. Neither the intercept nor the B coefficients enter either penalty term. P1 and the P2 diagonal are zero for baseline columns and one for genes. Artificial column multiplication is not used. M4 also fits the baseline without penalty. Being a baseline does not mean that its coefficient remains numerically fixed after genes are added; coefficients may change because features are correlated.

Gene-block lambda grid: `{0.0001, 0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1}`; rho: `{0, 0.5, 1}`. These are glum objective coefficients with mean loss; no formal conversion from the former sklearn C is assumed. For M3 and M5, the model without a gene block is also an inner-tuning candidate. For AUROC ties within 1e−6, prefer the no-gene candidate, then larger lambda, then larger rho. This does not make external deterioration impossible; selection takes place only in GEO.

Lambda-boundary review is bounded in advance: if the selected candidate has a nonzero gene block and lies at the lower/upper grid boundary, add one decade in that direction using the same inner CV, for at most **two rounds** per tuning procedure. A no-gene candidate or an entirely zero block is not a gene-boundary selection. Criterion, folds and tie rule do not change. Expansion counts and remaining boundaries are reported; a remaining boundary does not authorize unlimited expansion or use of PPMI. The same rule applies to the auxiliary sex model with its own grid; the age ridge grid remains its fixed list.

Solver under the numerical amendment before locking: use the L2-specific `irls-ls` when `rho=0` and `lambda>0`; use `irls-cd` for other candidates and baseline-only. Objective and penalties are unchanged. Initial settings: `max_iter=1000`, `max_inner_iter=100000`, `gradient_tol=1e−7`, `scale_predictors=False`, cyclic selection and master seed 20261014. Input scaling is independently fitted using training data only. Baseline columns with zero training variance or exact linear dependence are removed in a fixed column-name order with a rank check; the same selection is applied to test data and is identical for M4/M5. Inner-loop warnings are recorded but alone do not constitute final failure. The numerical section below specifies KKT and one fixed retry; invalid folds are not removed and the dependent analysis is not declared valid from an average of the remaining folds.

Gene selection requires positive MAD and selects up to 1,000 genes in each training partition, including inner folds. In multisource training, a gene's score is the minimum MAD across training sources. Ties are broken by gene_id. Age is standardized using training data before age² and the interaction are constructed; constructed columns are also standardized using training data only. No disease-severity variable, olfaction, batch or cohort ID enters the five primary models.

### Final KKT criterion and consequences

The complete objective and gradient are defined in [step zero](KKT_STEP_ZERO_v3_1_EN.md), and the solver change in the [16 October amendment](KKT_NUMERICAL_AMENDMENT_20261016_EN.md). KKT uses inputs standardized only with training data, loss divided by the sum of weights, an unpenalized intercept and the same P1/P2 vectors; full deviance with a factor of two is not substituted for NLL. P2 is diagonal only. Exact-zero coefficients use the subgradient rule; nonzero coefficients use the gradient plus L1/L2. Small coefficients are not zeroed to improve KKT.

Numerical acceptance requires finite outputs and `max(abs(KKT residual)) <= 1e-6`; the L1 norm is also reported. This threshold was checked in 48 comparisons with independent SciPy and separate calibration/check seeds, with 40 and 1000 genes and nonuniform sample/penalty weights; it was not changed after outputs. The deliberate factor-of-two error was detected. This criterion is not an AUROC-error bound.

If the initial fit fails the criterion, only one cold retry with `max_iter=5000,max_inner_iter=1000000,gradient_tol=1e-8` is allowed; model, data, weights and lambda/rho do not change. Warnings, stage, arm, rep, fold, candidate, solver, settings and KKT are logged for every attempt. A persistently invalid candidate is not removed from selection: the entire dependent tuning/analysis is invalid. Failure of the final M4 fit or final M5 tuning/fit makes the confirmatory comparison unevaluable; M4 has no hyperparameter. Failure of the sex control makes its gate unevaluable; an invalid age control is reported under its nonstopping role. Other independent analyses may continue.

The 1000-gene numerical stress test is a limited check of one final-training-like fixture; it does not guarantee convergence in every real fold and is not a third-platform transfer control. The null simulation's independent test also comes from the same two sources. Both limitations are explicitly reported.

## Six cell-marker panels

The four initial MCP-counter panels have T=14 genes, B=8, monocytic=7 and neutrophil=15. Two new panels were extracted from the [independent xCell reference](https://github.com/dviraran/xCell). For each type, genes present in at least half of that type's published signatures were selected before observing any performance; mapping to the shared universe is exact and unique:

| New panel | Reference signatures | Reference consensus genes | Usable shared genes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Erythroid, reference label Erythrocytes | 12 | 11 | 11 |
| Platelet | 3 | 123 | 118 |

For each panel, the mean rank percentile over its fixed genes is computed within each sample. Lists and reference hashes are recorded in the [manifest](v3_marker_manifest.json). These scores are not actual cell fractions, CBC measurements, the full MCP-counter algorithm or the full xCell algorithm. Marker scores can reflect cell activation and sample preparation as well as abundance. Even six panels do not guarantee complete coverage of cell composition. Erythroid scores are sensitive to globin removal/processing and may also proxy platform; adjustment is not described as complete removal of cellular confounding.

## Bounded controls and prespecified consequences

**Linkage and sex:** the documented IR3 crosswalk establishes identifier linkage; sex concordance does not replace it. The sex control uses the five available Y markers (`KDM5D, DDX3Y, RPS4Y1, UTY, EIF1AY`) and ridge logistic regression trained only in GEO controls. It uses the same backend, the gene lambda grid above and rho=0, selected by inner AUROC. Each GEO transfer direction targets AUROC≥0.95; in PPMI controls, AUROC≥0.95 and a CI lower bound≥0.90 define operational sex transfer. Balanced accuracy at a fixed threshold of 0.5 is descriptive only; a cross-platform probability offset alone does not close the gate. Uncertainty from small samples is reported separately. XIST is absent from the current list; no additional QC panel is added because of this. Labels are not overwritten using predictions and no record is excluded solely for model discordance.

**Age:** ridge regression using autosomal genes, trained only in GEO controls; alpha `{0.01,0.1,1,10,100}`, MAE tuning, ties favor larger alpha. Spearman and MAE are reported against a constant training-median-age predictor. The age control has demonstrated development capacity only if both GEO transfer directions have Spearman≥0.4 and MAE better than baseline; otherwise, external failure is not called evidence of a PPMI problem. This does not guarantee transfer of all autosomal PD signals. Sex/age results are not used to modify the pipeline and then present the same PPMI as an untouched test of the new method.

**CBC:** the Blood_Chemistry/Hematology table was identified in the PPMI guide, but its file has not been received in the project. If accessible, prefer the same blood-draw day, otherwise the nearest record within ±30 days; time gaps, units, participant counts and missingness are reported. Associations of neutrophil and monocytic scores with corresponding counts/percentages and platelet score with platelet count are examined; for erythroid, ordinary RBC counts are not assumed to substitute for reticulocytes. Total lymphocytes are not specific T- or B-cell validation.

Primary CBC analysis is in **healthy controls**; PD is reported separately as exploratory, and pooled correlation across both groups is not the validation criterion. For supportive evidence for each panel with its corresponding measurement, the descriptive rule is n≥30, Spearman≥0.4 and CI lower bound>0; this is not a universal biological standard. If evidence is weak, uncertain or unavailable, M4/M5 remain **the same models**, and the baseline is described as containing marker scores with insufficient/partial cell validation. This is not a stopping gate or permission to select panels by results; it only limits the claim. CBC is not a new predictor in the primary comparison.

**Longitudinal similarity:** diagnostic only. Using the fixed autosomal genes in the cache, construct within-sample ranks under the same rule; separately for this diagnostic, subtract each gene's mean across QC-pass samples from the release and calculate cosine similarity. Compare visits sharing a PATNO with other PATNOs of the same recorded sex, additionally matching processing wave if sample size permits, and summarize at donor level; report numbers of donors with multiple visits and insufficient strata. This centering is only an identity diagnostic and does not enter the PD model or inductive transformation. No similarity threshold automatically excludes or reassigns a sample; low similarity can reflect time, treatment or biological change. This control does not substitute for genetic identity matching.

**Stopping/suspension exit rule:** unresolved unique-link ambiguity, transformation error or failure of the PPMI sex gate stops confirmatory PD execution/interpretation. If the sex control did not first achieve AUROC≥0.95 in both GEO transfer directions, the gate has insufficient development support and the confirmatory execution prespecified as conditional on it does not proceed; this is not evidence of PPMI failure. Following failure, only a technical bug supported by independent, demonstrable evidence can justify a timestamped amendment followed by repetition of **the same fixed control**; all runs are recorded. Without such an explanation, the confirmatory outcome is "unevaluable under the protocol"; no particular biological/technical cause is asserted and no alternative transformation is chosen to rescue this holdout. A different method requires a new study/registration and independent validation. Age failure/limited capacity, weak or unavailable CBC and uncertain longitudinal similarity only limit reporting/claims and do not automatically exclude or reassign samples. No external control score is used to tune disease prediction.

## Evaluation and endpoint

Within each GEO: donor-level nested CV with five outer and five inner folds, stratified by class. Two directional transfers, PROBE→GENEPARK-TRO and the reverse, with tuning only in the training source. Final fitting uses 381 development records with inner CV stratified by cohort×class, the fixed weighting rule and within-source macro criterion. All models use the same splits, with gene selection/scaling inside each fit. This study has two development sources and one external test; it is not LOCO with three or more development sources.

**No-gene branch:** if development selects the no-gene candidate or the final fit's gene block is zero, M5 is by definition M4; score it with the same saved baseline fit and preprocessing so no merely numerical difference is manufactured. This status is recorded before any PPMI score. In this branch, Δ=0 and CI=[0,0] for identical models are tautological, not evidence excluding potential incremental gene value at a margin of 0.05. The primary result is "the gene block was not selected in development"; the confirmatory margin rule is not used to infer gene value, and no replacement model/endpoint is selected after results.

**Sole confirmatory comparison in the distinct-model branch:** `AUROC(M5)−AUROC(M4)` in PPMI. M3−M1 and other comparisons are secondary. Primary CI: 5,000 paired donor-bootstrap repetitions stratified by PD/control, a 95% percentile CI, fixed developed models and common indices across models. It does not include all training uncertainty. DeLong is not an alternative confirmatory decision. Before external scoring, bootstrap behavior for ties/identical models and synthetic-data coverage will be checked and reported; a material failure requires an amendment, not selective CI switching after results.

Report AUROC and AUPRC with the sample case fraction and descriptive calibration; balanced accuracy for PD models is optional appendix material only. Case/control model probabilities are not called population clinical risk. Metrics are reported by source with macro summaries; pooled AUC across the two GEO sources is not an endpoint.

Sensitivities are limited to X/Y removal, training-only age/sex residualization, GEO and PPMI age/sex matching with a three-year caliper without replacement, removal of the six panels' genes from the added M5 block, development technical-metadata controls and the v2 development ComBat audit. Matched PPMI evaluation uses the same models without refitting/tuning and a matched-pair bootstrap unit. The 1,000 development permutations rerun y-dependent stages within cohort×outer-fold blocks and are a secondary negative control only. No new sensitivity is added after results to replace the endpoint.

## Precision and inconclusive outcomes

**δ*=0.05** AUROC improvement is the research-relevance margin because the study should assess whether at least a five-percentage-point improvement over a strong baseline is compatible with the data; it is not a validated threshold of clinical benefit. Smaller effects are still reported with estimates and CIs.

The design-risk criticism is accepted: if estimated Δ is near zero and the approximately symmetric CI half-width is around 0.055, the CI does not exclude an increase of 0.05. The v2 simulation showed this limitation in some low/moderate-correlation scenarios. **Excluding an increase ≥0.05 with appropriate confidence is feasible in only part of the design scenarios; an inconclusive result is legitimate and accepted in advance.** Half-width alone does not determine every real run's conclusion; the final reporting condition is CI upper bound<0.05. If the CI covers both zero and 0.05, the result is inconclusive. These rules do not become a claim excluding gene value in the M5≡M4 branch. For an approximately truly zero center, half-width<0.05 is a useful precision guide, not a guarantee of power or absolute impossibility of rejection in a random sample.

The v2 table of 31% to 79% is an approximation under fixed Gaussian scores; scenario probabilities are unknown, and this is not a prediction of results or bootstrap-power validation. Even a negative result with appropriate controls shows only a limitation of this transfer procedure; it does not prove absence of biological PD signal. The paper's value depends on transfer controls, a fair comparison and reporting precision, rather than merely obtaining positive Δ.

## Preregistration, scope closure and execution order

Before the **first score in PPMI**, including sex, age and cell-marker scores, a timestamped public version in **Zenodo with a DOI**, and an exact Git commit/tag if used, is required. A local file, hash or local tag alone does not satisfy this. Prior linkage and metadata exposure are disclosed. The registration-ready package contains only method text, config, synthetic code, reference gene sets and hashes; no PPMI individual CSV, cache, identifier or expression vector is included. Public registration **has not yet occurred** at this stage.

After registration: environment/pipeline preparation; numerical/linkage checks and the listed controls; development and final-model locking; finally, one execution of the external PD package. Medication at sampling and QC-Fail rates by group, with pre-QC denominators, are reported as prespecified data audits. Overall archive counts do not substitute for those tables.

**Scope is closed from v3.1 onward.** A new critique alone does not create a new control. Only an error in identifiers/donor independence, feature transformation, leakage, penalties, convergence, eligibility or inference that changes the reliability of the confirmatory result requires a timestamped amendment and targeted check. Weak or inconclusive results do not justify changes to models, margin, population or controls. Post-result exploratory findings are labeled accordingly.
